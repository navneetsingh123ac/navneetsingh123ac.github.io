Tilt Box Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

In short: Tilt Box has no accounts of its own and does not ask for your name, email or location. Your progress is saved on your device and, if you use Google Play Games, in your Google Play Games account. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device information to show and measure those ads.

Who we are

Tilt Box ("the game") is made by Sketchify Studio, an independent game studio. In this policy, "we" means the developer of Tilt Box. You can contact us at navneet@sketchifystudio.com.

Information the game stores on your device

The game saves your level progress, stars, coins, hint and undo tokens, shelf items, daily puzzle and gift calendar progress, Weekend Tower progress, your settings and simple gameplay counters (for example, how many levels you have won) in the app's private storage on your device. This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Google Play Games (cloud save)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game can save a copy of the same progress to your Google Play Games account, so you can continue on another device or after reinstalling. This copy is stored by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; we do not receive it. You can manage or delete Play Games data in the Google Play Games app or your Google Account settings.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The game has no sign-up and no chat. If we add anonymous gameplay analytics (for example, which levels are too hard) in a later version, we will describe it here before it is used.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for example, for extra moves, a hint, or to double a chest) and occasional ads between levels. Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and ad interactions to serve, personalize and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy options in the game's Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Notifications

After you solve your first daily puzzle, the game asks whether you want a reminder when the next one is ready. If you say yes, it schedules at most one local notification a day on your device. Nothing is sent to a server. You can turn reminders off in the game's Settings or in your device's notification settings.

Permissions (Android)

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and, if you use it, Google Play Games cloud save. The game itself works offline.
  • Notifications: only for the optional daily puzzle reminder.
  • Vibration: for short haptic feedback during play.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob for advertising, as described above.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data processed by Google AdMob or Google Play Games, you can use the controls described above or contact Google directly.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: navneet@sketchifystudio.com
