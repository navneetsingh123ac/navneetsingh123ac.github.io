Swarmbreaker Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

In short: Swarmbreaker has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or location. Your progress is saved only on your device. The game shows ads, and the ad provider (Google AdMob in the Android app, CrazyGames on the web) may collect device information to show and measure those ads.

Who we are

Swarmbreaker ("the game") is an independent game. In this policy, "we" means the developer of Swarmbreaker. You can contact us at navneet@sketchifystudio.com.

Information the game stores on your device

The game saves your coins, unlocked heroes and skins, purchased power-ups, best times, daily reward streak and sound settings in your device's local storage. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

On CrazyGames, if you are signed in, CrazyGames may back up this saved progress to your CrazyGames account so you can continue on other devices. That backup is handled by CrazyGames under their privacy policy.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The game has no sign-up, no chat and no analytics of its own.

Advertising

Android app: the app uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded ads (for example, to revive or double your coins) and occasional ads between runs. Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and ad interactions to serve, personalize and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy options button in the game's main menu. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Web version on CrazyGames: ads are provided by CrazyGames and its partners under the CrazyGames privacy policy.

Permissions (Android)

  • Internet and network state: to load ads. The game itself works offline.
  • Vibration: for short haptic feedback during play.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob for advertising, as described above.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data processed by Google AdMob or CrazyGames, you can use the controls described above or contact those companies directly.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: navneet@sketchifystudio.com
