About the game

Move, dodge, choose. The weapons do the rest.

Your weapons fire on their own. Every level-up offers three upgrade cards: stack Magic Bolts, Orbit Blades, Chain Zap, Fire Aura, Boomerang Axes, Frost Nova and Meteors into wild builds.

Max out a weapon and pair it with the right item to evolve it into a super weapon such as Arcane Barrage, Thunder God, Absolute Zero or Armageddon.

  • 4 heroesEach starts with a different weapon and playstyle.
  • 7 weapon evolutionsCombine a maxed weapon with the right item.
  • Elites and bossesTreasure-dropping elites, a boss at 4:00 and a final boss at 8:00.
  • Long-term progressionPermanent power-ups, 7 hero skins and growing daily rewards.
  • One-finger controlsDrag anywhere to move. Built for phones.
  • Plays offlineNo account needed. Progress is saved on your device.
