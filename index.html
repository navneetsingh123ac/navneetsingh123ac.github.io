The studio

Small studio. Serious about play.

Sketchify Studio is an independent developer. Every game starts as a rough sketch and is played, measured and refined until the moment-to-moment feel is right.

  • Instant to play

    Simple controls and short sessions. Players are in the action within seconds.

  • Built for real devices

    Performance is tested on everyday phones, and our games run offline.

  • Respect for players

    No accounts and no personal data collection. Progress stays on the player's device.
