Monster Muscle Gym Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

In short: Monster Muscle Gym has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or location. Your gym and progress are saved only on your device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device information to show and measure those ads.

Who we are

Monster Muscle Gym ("the game") is made by Sketchify Studio, an independent game studio. In this policy, "we" means the developer of Monster Muscle Gym. You can contact us at navneet@sketchifystudio.com.

Information the game stores on your device

The game saves your gym, cash, machines, staff, monster collection, trophies, outfits, daily goals, the optional gym nickname you type, and your sound settings in your device's local storage. It also keeps simple gameplay counters (for example, how many fights you have won) on the device to power milestones. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The game has no sign-up, no chat and no online analytics of its own.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded ads (for example, to double your offline earnings or speed up a delivery) and occasional ads at natural breaks, such as moving to a new city. Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and ad interactions to serve, personalize and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time with Ad privacy options in the game's Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Permissions (Android)

  • Internet and network state: to load ads. The game itself works offline.
  • Vibration: for short haptic feedback during play.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob for advertising, as described above.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data processed by Google AdMob, you can use the controls described above or contact Google directly.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: navneet@sketchifystudio.com
