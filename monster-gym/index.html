About the game

Check in. Work out. Get SWOLE.

Blobs, slimes, sharks and golems line up at your desk. Send them to the treadmills, dumbbells, punching bags and battle ropes, collect the cash and keep building.

Hire staff to run the gym for you, train your regulars into champions for the Fight Arena, then max out Downtown and open a brand-new gym on the Beach.

  • 8 machine typesBuild and upgrade them to work faster and pay more.
  • A staff that works for youReceptionist, cleaner, barista and manager keep the gym running.
  • Fight for the beltBeat the Challengers in the ring and win the Title Fight.
  • New citiesDowntown, then the open-air Beach with its tides, then the World Tour.
  • Daily goals and milestonesEarn trophies and spend them on outfits in the Trophy Shop.
  • Earns while you're awayOffline earnings, and no account needed. Plays offline.
